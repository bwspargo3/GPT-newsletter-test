Market-value adjusted annuities: Key considerations for advisors
InsuranceNewsNet  ·  2026-07-31
• Market-value adjusted (MVA) fixed annuities offer potentially higher interest rates, but withdrawals above the free limit during the surrender period may be adjusted based on interest rate changes. • If interest rates have declined since purchase, an early withdrawal may result in a positive adjustment, while an increase in interest rates may result in a negative adjustment. • MVA fixed annuities may be suitable for clients with a longer time horizon who don't anticipate needing access to funds during the surrender period, according to Tom Buckingham, chief growth officer at Nassau Financial Group.
Why it matters
This information is relevant to L&A consulting actuaries working on product development and reserving, as it highlights the trade-offs and considerations for MVA fixed annuities that can impact policy design, pricing, and reserve calculations.
